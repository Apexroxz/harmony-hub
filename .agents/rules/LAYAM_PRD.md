# Layam Music Player — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. PRODUCT VISION
Layam is a hybrid music player supporting seamless offline (local files) and online streaming.

## 2. CURRENT PHASE (PHASE 1 - MVP)
- Simple Audio Player (MP3/FLAC playback, play/pause, volume, seek).
- Offline / Online Toggle Switch in header.
- Local Library viewer.
- Simple Song Upload interface.

## 3. WHAT IS FROZEN / HIDDEN FOR NOW
- Complex Web3 wallet locking logic.
- Dummy analytics metrics.
- Unnecessary promotional landing sections.
